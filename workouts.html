01

Monday

Lower Body Strength + Run

▾
🔥 Warmup — 10 Min

Activates glutes, opens hips, warms knees and ankles before heavy squatting and running.
🏃 Morning Run
  • Steady run at moderate (conversational) pace3 miles
🏋️ Lifting
  • Back Squat4×6 @ 80% 1RM
  • Romanian Deadlift3×8
  • Leg Press3×10
  • Walking Lunges3×12 each leg
  • Calf Raises4×15

🦵 Joint & Mobility Work — 10 Min Post-Lift
  • 90/90 Hip Stretch2×60 sec each side
  • Knee CARs (Controlled Articular Rotations)2×10 each leg
  • Tibialis Raises (shin/ankle health)3×20
  • Ankle Circles2×15 each direction
  • Adductor Rockbacks2×10
Knees and hips take the brunt of squats, lunges, and rucking. These keep joint capsules healthy and reduce injury risk.

Tuesday

Upper Body Strength — Push

▾
🔥 Warmup — 10 Min

  • Arm Circles (small to large)10 each direction

Shoulder injuries are extremely common in soldiers from carrying loads. These bulletproof your rotator cuff and thoracic mobility.

Interval Run + Hip & Knee Prehab

▾
🔥 Warmup — 10 Min
  • Easy jog5 min
  • High Knees2×20 sec
  • Butt Kicks2×20 sec

  • B-Skip2×20 meters

Running warmups should be dynamic and movement-based. Skips and carioca groove proper running mechanics and get hips and ankles firing before intervals.
🏃 Interval Run
  • Warm-up jog1 mile
  • 400m repeats at hard effort (90 sec rest between)6 reps
  • Cool-down jog1 mile
🦵 Post-Run Prehab Circuit — 10 Min
  • Clamshells with Band3×15 each side
  • Single-Leg Glute Bridges3×12 each side
  • Copenhagen Planks3×8 each side
  • Standing Hip Flexor March2×20
  • Eccentric Calf Drops (on step)3×15 each leg
Runners and ruckers are prone to IT band issues, runner's knee, and Achilles problems. This circuit targets weak links before they become injuries.

Thursday

Upper Body Strength — Pull + Neck & Spine

▾
🔥 Warmup — 10 Min
  • Row or jump rope5 min
  • Dead Hang from Bar (decompress spine)2×20 sec
  • Scapular Pull-Ups (retraction only, no arm bend)2×12
  • Band Pull-Aparts2×15
  • Face Pulls with Band2×15

  • Neck Isometrics (light, front/back/sides)5 sec hold × 3 each
  • Hip Hinge Practice (bodyweight, slow)2×10
Pull days demand healthy shoulders, mobile thoracic spine, and prepared posterior chain. The dead hang alone is one of the best shoulder warmups in existence.
🏋️ Lifting
  • Deadlift4×5 @ 85% 1RM
  • Weighted Pull-Ups4×6
  • Barbell Rows4×8
  • Dumbbell Rows3×10
  • Face Pulls3×15
  • Barbell or Dumbbell Curls3×12
🧠 Joint & Mobility — Neck & Spine Focus
  • Neck CARs (slow, full range)2×5 each direction
  • Neck Isometrics (front, back, each side)3×10 sec holds
  • Cat-Cow2×15
  • Child's Pose with Lat Reach2×45 sec each side
  • Dead Hang from Pull-Up Bar3×30–45 sec
  • Thoracic Extension over Foam Roller2×60 sec
Rucking loads compress the spine and strain the neck and traps. Dead hangs decompress the spine. Neck work prevents strain from heavy pack straps.

🎒 Ruck Progression
Week Distance Weight Goal
1–22 miles25 lbsBuild base
3–43 miles30 lbsAdd distance
5–64 miles35 lbsAdd weight
7–85 miles40 lbsArmy standard
9+6–8 miles45–50 lbsElite level
🎒 Ruck Form Tips
  • Keep chest tall — do not hunch forward into the pack
  • Drive through the heel with each step
  • Brace your core throughout like you're taking a punch
  • Pack weight should ride high and close to your back
  • Use trekking poles on long rucks to protect the knees
🦶 Post-Ruck Recovery — 10 Min
  • Standing Quad Stretch60 sec each leg
  • Pigeon Pose60 sec each side
  • Standing Calf Stretch on Step45 sec each leg
  • Thoracic Foam Roll90 sec
  • Plantar Fascia Roll (tennis/lacrosse ball)60 sec each foot
Rucking hammers your feet, plantar fascia, calves, and lower back. This routine flushes out the damage before it accumulates.

Joint Supplement Stack
  • Creatine Monohydrate5g/day — strength and cellular hydration
  • Fish Oil / Omega-3s2–3g/day — reduces joint inflammation
  • Collagen Peptides + Vitamin C30–60 min pre-training — directly feeds connective tissue repair
  • Magnesium Glycinate300–400mg before bed — muscle recovery and sleep quality
  • Vitamin D3 + K22,000–5,000 IU/day — bone density and immune health
Nutrition Guidelines

Macros

  • Protein1g per lb of bodyweight — chicken, eggs, beef, fish, Greek yogurt
  • CarbohydratesFuel runs and rucks — oats, rice, potatoes, fruit
  • FatsAvocado, nuts, olive oil — joint and hormone support

Hydration & Recovery Foods

  • Water1 gallon minimum daily — more on ruck days
  • Bone BrothRegular intake for tendon and ligament health
  • GelatinEat regularly to support joint collagen production
Progression Rules
  • Add 5 lbs to lower body lifts every week
  • Add 2.5 lbs to upper body lifts every week
  • Add 0.5 miles to your long run every 2 weeks
  • Follow the ruck progression table on Friday — distance before weight
  • Deload every 5th week — drop weight by 40%, keep all warmups and mobility work
  • Track your lifts — progress only happens if you measure it
Key Reminders
Never Skip the WarmupCold muscles and joints are how careers end. Every session, no exceptions.
Mobility is TrainingTreat it with the same seriousness as lifting. It's what keeps you in the game for years, not months.
Rucking is a SkillForm before weight, always. A bad ruck posture over miles destroys your knees and spine.
Sleep 7–9 HoursThis is where actual adaptation happens. No sleep = no gains, no recovery, no performance.
Listen to Your JointsSharp pain means stop immediately. Soreness means adapt. Know the difference.
Stay Consistent 90 DaysThis is when real transformation shows. Trust the process and execute every single day.
